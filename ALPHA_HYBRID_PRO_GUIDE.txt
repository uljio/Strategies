═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📊 OVERVIEW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ALPHA HYBRID PRO is a confluence-based trading strategy that combines the
most effective non-repainting components from multiple proven strategies in
this repository. It's designed for medium-to-high frequency trading on crypto,
forex, and stock markets.

🎯 KEY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ 100% NON-REPAINTING - All HTF analysis uses proper lookahead settings
✅ MULTI-INDICATOR CONFLUENCE - Requires multiple confirmations for entries
✅ DYNAMIC RISK MANAGEMENT - ATR-based stops adapt to volatility
✅ PARTIAL PROFIT-TAKING - 3-tier exits (TP1/TP2/TP3) maximize winners
✅ DAILY LIMITS - Prevents overtrading and protects profits
✅ SESSION FILTERING - Trade only during optimal market hours
✅ FLEXIBLE POSITION SIZING - USD-based or SL-based risk management

🔧 COMPONENTS EXPLAINED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. HTF ALMA TREND FILTER
   - Higher timeframe ALMA (Arnaud Legoux Moving Average)
   - Non-repainting implementation using lookahead_on
   - Filters out counter-trend trades
   - Default: 60min timeframe, 50 period

2. GUPPY EMA RIBBONS
   - Fast EMAs (3-23) and Slow EMAs (25-70)
   - Requires proper alignment for entries
   - Identifies strong trending conditions
   - Lookback filter prevents false signals

3. VWAP FILTER
   - Volume-weighted average price
   - Only long above VWAP, only short below
   - Aligns with institutional money flow

4. SUPERTREND
   - Volatility-based trend indicator
   - Provides entry triggers on crossovers
   - Default: 2.0 factor, 10 ATR length

5. EMA 200 FILTER
   - Classic trend filter
   - Optional but recommended
   - Only long above, only short below

6. ATR-BASED STOPS
   - Adapts to market volatility
   - Default: 1.5x ATR multiplier
   - Prevents tight stops in volatile markets

7. MULTI-LEVEL EXITS
   - TP1: 40% at 1.5R (quick profit)
   - TP2: 35% at 2.5R (mid-range)
   - TP3: 25% at 4.0R (let winners run)

💡 USAGE TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. START WITH DEFAULTS
   - Don't optimize immediately
   - Test on multiple pairs/assets
   - Run at least 6-12 months of backtest data

2. ENABLE ALL FILTERS INITIALLY
   - Start with maximum confluence
   - Gradually disable filters if too few trades
   - Never disable HTF filter (most important)

3. ADJUST FOR YOUR MARKET
   - Crypto: Use wider stops (higher volatility)
   - Forex: Can use tighter stops
   - Stocks: Consider session filters for market hours

4. POSITION SIZING
   - SL-Based sizing: More consistent risk per trade
   - Fixed USD: Easier to understand, but risk varies
   - Start conservative (0.5-1% risk)

5. DAILY LIMITS
   - Max Trades: Prevents overtrading
   - Max Profit: Locks in good days
   - Recommended: 10 trades, $500 profit (adjust to capital)

6. SESSION FILTERS
   - Crypto: Can trade 24/7, but consider major sessions
   - Forex: NY (1330-1600 UTC) and London (0800-1100 UTC)
   - Stocks: Market hours only (0930-1600 local)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROBLEM: No trades being taken
SOLUTION:
- Disable some filters (start with EMA 200)
- Check date range is correct
- Verify session filter settings
- Check daily limits haven't been hit

PROBLEM: Too many trades
SOLUTION:
- Enable more filters (especially EMA 200)
- Increase Guppy lookback period
- Add session filter
- Lower max daily trades

PROBLEM: Frequent stop-outs
SOLUTION:
- Increase ATR multiplier for SL
- Check if market is too choppy (avoid ranging markets)
- Verify Guppy alignment is working
- Consider using EMA 200 filter

PROBLEM: Missing big moves
SOLUTION:
- Reduce TP quantities for TP1/TP2
- Increase TP3 R:R ratio
- Check HTF timeframe isn't too high
- Verify Guppy lookback isn't too strict

PROBLEM: Strategy not matching backtest
SOLUTION:
- Verify all settings match exactly
- Check for repainting (shouldn't happen with this strategy)
- Ensure using same timeframe and HTF setting
- Consider market regime change

🎓 ADVANCED TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. MULTI-ASSET PORTFOLIO
   - Run on 3-5 uncorrelated assets
   - Reduces overall portfolio drawdown
   - Spreads risk across markets

2. TIME-BASED OPTIMIZATION
   - Some hours perform better than others
   - For crypto: 12:00-20:00 UTC often best
   - For forex: Overlap sessions (12:00-16:00 UTC)

3. VOLATILITY ADJUSTMENT
   - In low volatility: Reduce ATR multiplier
   - In high volatility: Increase ATR multiplier
   - Consider VIX for stocks, ATR percentile for crypto

4. CORRELATION WITH MAJOR INDICES
   - Most altcoins follow BTC
   - Consider BTC trend when trading alts
   - Use BTC HTF filter for altcoin trades

5. NEWS AVOIDANCE
   - Major economic news can cause whipsaws
   - Consider disabling trading 30min before/after:
     * FOMC announcements
     * NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls)
     * CPI releases
